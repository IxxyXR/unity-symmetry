# CCTBX

SpaceGroupDomainData.cs contains closed asymmetric-unit outlines derived from the CCTBX direct-space asymmetric-unit reference table. Shared-boundary ownership conditions are omitted for visualization. Group 4 uses a custom equivalent half-cell box.
Source: https://github.com/cctbx/cctbx_project/tree/a918c3666b2065fc68b927c0f5c903a7f9042395/cctbx/sgtbx/direct_space_asu
